# Design

An editorial workbench, inspired by a printed field manual: warm paper, deep ink, oxblood accent, fine rules, a strong serif headline and compact readable sans-serif controls. The product is operated, not marketed: a concise introduction leads straight to the file and configuration workspace.

Use a large typographic masthead, one continuous two-column workspace rather than nested cards, and a quiet results ledger. Source status and processing state are always visible. Native radio buttons, checkboxes and buttons provide understandable interaction. No game screenshots or ornamental statistics.

Self-host Fraunces and Source Sans 3. Keep touch targets at least 44px, contrast at least WCAG AA, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and no horizontal overflow at 360px. Desktop and mobile inspection happens in one bounded round; repair observed failures once, then confirm.

Impeccable's product-context, craft-floor and technical audit guidance informed the design. Evaluation distinguishes deterministic findings from visual judgment and records implementation limits.
