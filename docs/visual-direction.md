## Character background

Lu Bu, styled Fengxian, served Ding Yuan and later Dong Zhuo. The translated _Records of the Three Kingdoms_, scroll 7, describes his riding and archery, the nickname “Flying General,” his killing of Dong Zhuo, and his horse Red Hare. These are historical-literary context, not a specification for the game's costume. See the [Sanguozhi biography translation](https://kongming.net/biographies/sanguozhi/Lu-Bu/LadyWu).

The game's identifiable character design takes priority over generic historical armor. The [official Remastered character page](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw3-re/us/characters/) and its [Lu Bu portrait](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw3-re/assets/img/chara_pop-ryofu.jpg) show paired long curved helmet plumes, gold beast-shaped shoulder armor, green forearm armor, crimson armor edging, a purple waist accent, and a long polearm. These details are observed visual references, not claims of historical authenticity. The [official game overview](https://www.koeitecmoamerica.com/dw3-re/) identifies Lu Bu among the officers whose stories were added in Xtreme Legends.
